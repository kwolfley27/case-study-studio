Miso for Case Study Studio

Install (once):
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it.
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the miso-extension folder.
   Chrome warns it can "read data on all websites": that's what lets Miso take a picture of the tab you circle in.
   Miso only runs on Figma and Case Study Studio.

Use:
- Keep Case Study Studio open in a tab (it can stay in the background) and wake Miso there.
- In Figma, click the frame you want (so Miso gets its link). Hold Option and move the mouse in a loop around it,
  then let go of Option. No clicking. Miso holds up a little picture of it.
- Say what it's for: "Miso, make this the hero" or "Miso, put this in the final screens".
  The studio does it in the background and Miso answers right there on Figma.
- The first time, Chrome asks figma.com for the microphone. Allow it.

The picture stays on your computer until it's placed in your case study.
