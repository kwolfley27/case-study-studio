Miso for Case Study Studio

Install (once):
1. Unzip this folder somewhere you'll keep it.
2. In Chrome, open chrome://extensions and switch on "Developer mode" (top right).
3. Click "Load unpacked" and choose the miso-extension folder.

Use:
- In Figma (in Chrome), click the frame you want, then hold Option and draw around it.
  Miso appears in the corner holding a little picture of it.
- Switch to Case Study Studio, point at the spot and say "Miso, put it here".
  The picture goes in right away, and the studio pulls the full-quality frame from Figma.

Nothing is uploaded: the picture stays in the extension until you place it or clear it.
